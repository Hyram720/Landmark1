Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [Effective date]

Ledger Credit is operated by Sun Marketing Enterprises LLC, 2435 Patton St, Camden, NJ 08104 ("we," "us"). Because we bill only after work has been performed, most clients never need a refund. This policy explains your cancellation rights, when refunds are given, and our refund processing fee.

1. Your 3-business-day right to cancel

You may cancel your Credit Services Agreement for any reason, without any penalty or obligation, before midnight of the 3rd business day after the date you sign it. We do not perform any services and do not charge you anything during this period.

To cancel, send a signed, dated copy of the Notice of Cancellation you received with your agreement, or any other written notice, to:

Sun Marketing Enterprises LLC 2435 Patton St, Camden, NJ 08104 Email: sunmarketingllc@gmail.com

If any payment was received from you before the cancellation period ended, it will be refunded in full. No fee of any kind is ever charged for cancelling within 3 business days.

2. Cancelling at any time after that

You may cancel at any time after the 3-business-day period by notifying us in writing (email is fine). There is no cancellation fee. After you cancel:

  • We stop all work that has not yet been performed.
  • You will not be charged for any work that was not completed before you cancelled.
  • Invoices for work already completed before you cancelled remain payable under your plan.

3. Full refunds (no fee)

We will refund the full amount, with no refund processing fee, if:

  • you were charged for work that was not performed, or not performed as described on the invoice;
  • you were charged during your 3-business-day cancellation period;
  • you were charged in error, charged twice, or charged more than the price stated in your agreement;
  • on the Pay Per Delete plan, an item you paid to have removed is reinserted on the same credit report within 90 days after removal; or
  • a refund is otherwise required by law.

4. Refund processing fee (return fee)

If you ask for a refund of a fee for a service that was performed as described (for example, a dispute letter that was prepared, approved by you, and mailed), we may approve the refund as a courtesy. Courtesy refunds are subject to a refund processing fee of $15 per refund request, deducted from the amount refunded. The fee will never exceed the amount being refunded.

The refund processing fee is never charged on cancellations within 3 business days, on charges for work not performed, or on any refund listed in Section 3.

5. How to request a refund

Email sunmarketingllc@gmail.com within 60 days of the payment, with your name, the invoice number, and the reason. We will respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original card used for payment, and usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your card issuer.

If you have a question about a charge, please contact us first. We will review it promptly.

6. No guarantee of results

We cannot and do not promise any particular result, score increase, or removal. A refund under this policy is not based on whether your credit score changes.
