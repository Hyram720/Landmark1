Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Credit Ledger (myledgercredit.com) is operated by Sun Marketing Enterprises LLC, 2435 Patton St, Camden, NJ 08104 ("we," "us," "our"). Because we comply strictly with the federal Credit Repair Organizations Act (CROA, 15 U.S.C. § 1679b(b)) and bill only after work has been fully performed, you are never charged upfront fees. This policy explains your statutory 3-business-day right to cancel, how our post-work $49.00 Initial Credit Audit & Setup Fee and ongoing plans are billed, and when refunds apply.

1. Your 3-business-day statutory right to cancel (CROA, 15 U.S.C. § 1679e)

Under federal law (15 U.S.C. § 1679e), you may cancel your Credit Services Agreement for any reason, without any penalty or obligation, at any time before midnight of the 3rd business day after the date on which you signed the contract.

  • $0.00 billed and no work started during the window: We do not perform any credit repair services and never charge you any fee (including the $49.00 Initial Credit Audit & Setup Fee) during your 3-business-day cancellation window.
  • 100% unconditional refund guarantee if any amount was ever collected: Although our system charges $0.00 during the 3-business-day window, if any payment were ever received during that window, it is refunded 100% in full with $0.00 fee within 5 business days.
  • How to cancel within 3 business days:
    1. 1-Click Online Cancellation: Log into your client portal at myledgercredit.com/portal and click Cancel Agreement; or
    2. Email or Mail Written Notice: Email fred@myledgercredit.com or mail/deliver a signed, dated copy of the duplicate Notice of Cancellation form below to:
NOTICE OF CANCELLATION (15 U.S.C. § 1679e) You may cancel this contract, without any penalty or obligation, at any time before midnight of the 3rd day which begins after the date the contract is signed by you. To cancel this contract, mail or deliver a signed, dated copy of this cancellation notice, or any other written notice to: Sun Marketing Enterprises LLC (Credit Ledger) 2435 Patton St, Camden, NJ 08104 Email: fred@myledgercredit.com before midnight on the 3rd business day after you signed your Credit Services Agreement. I hereby cancel this transaction, ______________________ [Date] ______________________ [Purchaser's Signature]

2. Cancelling anytime after the 3-business-day window ($0.00 cancellation fee)

You are never locked into a long-term contract. You may cancel your Monthly Membership ($9.99/mo), Annual Membership ($79.00/yr), or Pay-Per-Deletion agreement at any time after the 3-business-day period with $0.00 cancellation fee:

  • Cancel online in your client portal (/portal), or email fred@myledgercredit.com, or call (856) 655-7855.
  • Upon cancellation, we immediately stop all unperformed work and future dispute rounds.
  • You will never be billed for any future month, uncompleted service period, or unmailed dispute letter after you cancel.
  • Invoices for services that were already fully performed before you cancelled remain payable according to your agreement.

3. Automatic 100% full refunds ($0.00 fee)

We issue a 100% full refund, with no deduction or processing fee of any kind, whenever:

  • you cancel within your 3-business-day statutory cancellation period (if any amount was ever collected);
  • you were charged for a service, initial audit, or dispute deletion that was not actually performed or verified as described on your invoice;
  • you were billed in error, billed twice, billed after cancelling future service, or billed more than the price stated in your Credit Services Agreement;
  • under our Pay-Per-Deletion fee structure ($50–$150 per confirmed item removed, per bureau), a deleted item you paid for is reinserted onto the same credit bureau report within 90 days after deletion; or
  • a full refund is required by CROA, FCRA, New Jersey law, or applicable state law.

4. $49.00 One-Time Initial Credit Audit & Setup Fee & Completed Work Policy

  • Billed Strictly After Week-1 Performance (15 U.S.C. § 1679b(b)): The one-time Initial Credit Audit & Setup Fee of $49.00 is never charged at signup or during your 3-business-day cancellation window. It is billed only after your 3-business-day cancellation window has expired and your Week-1 setup and initial credit audit work (document collection and verification, 3-bureau credit report intake, and initial Metro 2 credit analysis and dispute roadmap) has been fully completed and delivered in your portal.
  • Non-Refundable Once Week-1 Work Is Performed & Billed: Because the $49.00 Initial Credit Audit & Setup Fee is charged only after the Week-1 setup and 3-bureau audit work has already been completed, once that Week-1 work is performed and billed, the $49.00 fee is non-refundable (except in the event of a billing error under Section 3). Your 3-business-day statutory right to cancel before any work is performed or billed remains 100% unconditional.
  • Completed Membership Periods & Verified Deletions: Monthly ($9.99/mo) and Annual ($79/yr) membership fees and Pay-Per-Deletion fees ($50–$150 per confirmed item deleted, per bureau) are billed strictly in arrears after the service period or verified bureau deletion is completed, and are non-refundable once performed except as provided in Section 3 (including the 90-day reinsertion refund guarantee on Pay-Per-Deletion items).

5. How to request a billing review or refund

To request a billing review or refund under Section 3, email fred@myledgercredit.com or call (856) 655-7855 within 60 days of the invoice payment with your name, invoice number, and reason. We respond within 2 to 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original credit or debit card used on Authorize.net and typically post within 5 to 10 business days depending on your card issuer.

6. No guarantee of credit score or removal outcomes

Under federal law (CROA), no credit repair organization may guarantee a specific credit score increase or the removal of accurate, current, and verifiable credit information. Eligibility for a refund under Section 3 is based on service performance and billing accuracy, not on third-party credit bureau or lender scoring outcomes.
