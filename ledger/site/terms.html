1. What we do

Credit Ledger is a credit repair organization as defined by the federal Credit Repair Organizations Act ("CROA," 15 U.S.C. § 1679 et seq.). We assist you to:

  • collect and verify your onboarding documents and perform a comprehensive Week-1 tri-bureau (Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion) Metro 2 compliance and statutory discrepancy audit;
  • identify specific items that you believe in good faith are inaccurate, incomplete, obsolete, or unverifiable;
  • prepare custom written dispute letters (under FCRA §§ 611 and 623) for your review and explicit approval before anything is sent;
  • mail approved dispute letters (including via USPS First-Class or Certified Mail through our print-and-mail partner, Lob.com) and track the 30-to-45-day statutory response deadlines required by federal law; and
  • report the investigation results to you in your secure client portal.

2. What we do not do (Strict Prohibitions)

  • No guarantee of specific results or score increases: We cannot and do not promise any specific credit score increase, timeframe, or removal of any item. Under federal law, accurate, current, and verifiable information cannot be removed from a credit report before the statutory reporting period expires (generally 7 years, or 10 years for Chapter 7 bankruptcy).
  • No tradelines, CPNs, or "new credit identities": We never sell, broker, or offer authorized-user tradelines ("piggybacking"), Credit Privacy Numbers (CPNs), EIN-for-SSN substitutions, or "new credit files."
  • No false statements or mass disputes of accurate items: We will never advise or assist you to make untrue or misleading statements to a credit reporting agency or creditor (15 U.S.C. § 1679b(a)(1)), or to dispute items you know are accurate.
  • Not a law firm or legal counsel: We do not provide legal advice or legal representation. If you need legal advice, please consult a licensed attorney.
  • No identity theft recovery services: If you believe you are a victim of identity theft, we refer you to IdentityTheft.gov, the Federal Trade Commission's free federal recovery resource.
  • No debt settlement, debt adjustment, or lending: We do not negotiate, settle, or adjust debts, we do not receive or hold client funds to pay creditors, and we do not lend money or extend credit.
