What Credit Ledger does

Review reports from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion and see where information may need a closer look.

  • Credit report review

    We run a Metro 2 audit to check for inconsistencies. Metro 2 is the standard format lenders use to report accounts to the bureaus.

  • Dispute letter preparation

    We prepare letters to the bureaus and companies that reported the information, for items you choose to dispute.

  • Track dispute progress

    See letter status and follow the work in your client portal.

How it works

  1. Enroll and review your rights

    Read the Consumer Credit File Rights statement and sign your agreement in the client portal.

  2. Upload your reports

    Share your reports through the portal so we can complete your initial credit report review.

  3. Approve letters and follow progress

    Review the dispute letters prepared for you and track the rounds you approve.
