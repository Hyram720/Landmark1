Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sun Marketing Enterprises LLC, doing business as Credit Ledger / Ledger Credit, 2435 Patton St, Camden, NJ 08104 ("we," "us," "our"), collects, uses, encrypts, shares, and retains your information when you visit myledgercredit.com, submit our public intake form (/apply or /checkout), or use our encrypted Client Portal (/portal).

1. Information we collect (separated by stage)

  • A. Public Intake Form (/apply and /checkout):
    • We collect only basic contact and inquiry details: your first and last name, email address, phone number, 2-letter state abbreviation, credit goals, how you heard about us (or referral partner code), and for business inquiries, your business legal name, entity type, and website.
    • Strict SSN / DOB Prohibition on Public Forms: Prospective clients are instructed not to submit their Social Security number (SSN), date of birth (DOB), credit card number, or full financial account numbers on /apply or /checkout.
  • B. Encrypted Client Portal (/portal — after enrollment & disclosures):
    • Truncated Identifier Storage Only: Your client profile in our database stores only the last 4 digits of your SSN (and last 4 digits of your EIN for business clients). Full Social Security numbers and dates of birth are never stored in our profile database.
    • Uploaded Verification Documents & Credit Reports: To process consumer disputes with Equifax, Experian, TransUnion, and creditors, you upload your government-issued photo ID, proof of current mailing address (such as a utility bill or bank statement), and 3-bureau credit reports (from AnnualCreditReport.com, IdentityIQ, MyFreeScoreNow, or SmartCredit) directly into your encrypted client portal. Every uploaded file is encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM.
  • C. Billing & Payment Information:
    • We store your selected billing plan, invoice records, and Authorize.net transaction status/IDs.
    • Card numbers never touch our servers: All card payments are entered directly on Authorize.net's Accept Hosted payment page (/pay/:token). We never receive, process, or store your full credit or debit card number or CVV.
  • D. Website Technical Logs:
    • Standard server security logs (IP address, request timestamp, browser user-agent, and rate-limiting counters) used strictly to prevent unauthorized access and abuse.

2. How we use your information

  • To deliver the statutory disclosures and Credit Services Agreement required by the Credit Repair Organizations Act (15 U.S.C. § 1679 et seq.).
  • To perform your Week-1 Initial Credit Audit & Setup, audit your credit reports for Metro 2 and FCRA discrepancies, and prepare custom written dispute letters for your review and approval.
  • To print and mail your approved dispute letters via USPS First-Class or Certified Mail through our fulfillment integration with Lob.com.
  • To send transactional account notifications, portal invitations, and post-performance invoices, and (only if you separately opt in) SMS status updates.
  • To maintain statutory compliance audit trails and 2-year CROA records.

3. Physical mail fulfillment via Lob.com & third-party sharing

We never sell or rent your personal information to third parties or data brokers. We share your information only as necessary to perform the services you expressly authorize:

  • Lob.com Physical Mail Integration: When (and only when) you review and approve a dispute letter in your client portal and authorize physical mailing, our server transmits over encrypted TLS to Lob.com:
    1. your name and return mailing address;
    2. the destination address of the credit reporting agency (Equifax, Experian, or TransUnion) or creditor/furnisher;
    3. the approved dispute letter PDF; and
    4. copies of your uploaded government photo ID and proof-of-address document attached as verification pages required by the credit bureaus.
    Lob.com uses this data solely as an operational print-and-mail processor to print, stuff, and mail your dispute via USPS First-Class Mail or USPS Certified Mail with Return Receipt and return the USPS tracking events to your portal.
  • Credit Bureaus & Data Furnishers: Receive the mailed dispute packets that you explicitly approve.
  • Authorize.net (and our Merchant Acquirer / ISO): Receives your invoice number, amount, name, and email when you open an invoice payment link to pay on Authorize.net's hosted checkout page.
  • Local Redaction Before Any Optional AI Analysis: When our optional AI report-scanning or letter-drafting tools are used, our local redaction engine (server/redact.js) automatically masks SSNs, EINs, dates of birth, card/account numbers, routing numbers, emails, phone numbers, street addresses, and client names before any text leaves our server, and blocks transmission if an unredacted SSN or card number is detected.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or regulatory examination.

4. TCPA SMS / text messaging privacy

If you opt in to receive SMS text messages from Credit Ledger, your mobile phone number and SMS opt-in consent data are used solely to send you messages about your Credit Ledger account. Mobile phone numbers and text-messaging opt-in consent data are never shared with or sold to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

5. How we protect your data

  • AES-256-GCM Encryption at Rest: Both our workspace database and every document uploaded to /portal are encrypted at rest on disk using AES-256-GCM derived from a server-only encryption key.
  • Encryption in Transit: All traffic between your browser, our servers, Authorize.net, and Lob.com is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access Controls & Audit Logging: Passwords are hashed using memory-hard scrypt, sessions use signed HttpOnly cookies, and every login, signature, file upload, file download, and workspace update is recorded in an owner-only security audit log.

6. Record retention (2-Year CROA Rule) & deletion

Under the federal Credit Repair Organizations Act (15 U.S.C. § 1679c(c)), we are legally required to retain copies of your signed consumer rights disclosure statement, Credit Services Agreement, and Notice of Cancellation for at least two (2) years after the date the disclosure statement is signed. Uploaded credit reports and identity documents may be deleted upon your written request once all active dispute rounds are closed, subject to statutory recordkeeping and tax/billing retention obligations.

7. Your privacy rights & choices

You may access, download, or request correction or deletion of your personal information (subject to the 2-year CROA retention requirement above) by logging into /portal or emailing fred@myledgercredit.com. We respond to all verifiable privacy requests within 30 days.

8. Adults 18+ only

Our website and credit repair services are intended solely for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

We will post any updates to this Privacy Policy on this page with an updated effective date.
