Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Effective date]

This Privacy Policy explains how Sun Marketing Enterprises LLC, doing business as Ledger Credit, 2435 Patton St, Camden, NJ 08104 ("we," "us"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use myledgercredit.com and our credit dispute services.

Information we collect

  • Contact information: name, email, phone number, mailing address, and state.
  • Identity information needed for disputes: such as date of birth, the last four digits or full Social Security number, and proof of identity and address, which credit bureaus require to process disputes.
  • Credit information: credit reports you provide or authorize us to obtain, and letters from credit bureaus and creditors.
  • Account and billing information: your plan, invoices, and payment status. Card numbers are entered only on Authorize.net's secure hosted payment page and are never received or stored by us.
  • Website information: basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To provide the services you request, including preparing and mailing disputes you approve.
  • To communicate with you about your account, invoices, and results.
  • To send text messages, only if you opt in.
  • To meet legal, tax, and recordkeeping requirements, and to prevent fraud.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with credit bureaus, creditors, and other recipients of disputes you approve;
  • with service providers that help us operate, such as our payment processor (Authorize.net), hosting, mail, email, and text message providers, who may use it only to provide services to us;
  • when required by law, subpoena, or to protect our legal rights; or
  • with your consent.

Mobile phone numbers and text messaging consent are not shared with third parties for their marketing.

How we protect it

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and limited staff access to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you of a security incident as required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to meet legal and recordkeeping requirements, then delete or de-identify it.

Your choices

You may request a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to legal recordkeeping requirements. You may opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP, and out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link.

Children

Our services are for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
