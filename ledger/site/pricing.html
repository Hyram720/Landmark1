Ledger Credit Pricing

You pay only after work is done. Ledger Credit never charges a setup fee, an enrollment fee, or any fee in advance. We do not bill you for anything until (1) your 3-business-day cancellation period has ended and (2) the service on the invoice has been fully performed.

Ledger Credit is a credit dispute service operated by Sun Marketing Enterprises LLC, 2435 Patton St, Camden, NJ 08104.

Choose one billing plan

You choose one plan when you enroll. Your plan and its prices are written into your Credit Services Agreement before you sign.

Plan Price When you are billed
Per Letter $49 per dispute letter After each dispute letter is prepared, approved by you, and mailed.
Pay Per Delete $75 per item removed or corrected Only after a credit bureau or creditor removes or corrects an item you disputed. If nothing is removed or corrected, you pay nothing.
Monthly After Service $99 per month of service One invoice per month, and only for a month in which we mailed at least one dispute letter for you. If no letters go out in a month, there is no charge for that month.

What is always included at no charge

  • Your initial credit consultation
  • Review of your credit reports with you
  • Credit file disclosure requests (requests for a copy of your file)
  • Goodwill adjustment request letters
  • Help preparing a consumer complaint for you to review and file yourself
  • Tracking of bureau and creditor response deadlines
  • Access to your secure client portal

Limits that protect you

  • 12-month agreement term. Each Credit Services Agreement runs for up to 12 months. It continues after that only if you renew in writing.
  • Maximum total. Your total charges under any plan will not exceed $1,188 during a 12-month agreement term (the same as 12 months on the Monthly plan). Your agreement states the total you may pay.
  • No membership fees and no installment plans. You are billed only for completed work, as described above.
  • No cancellation fee. You may cancel at any time. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

How payment works

  1. When a service is complete, we email you an invoice describing the work performed and the date it was completed.
  2. Each invoice includes a secure payment link. You pay on Authorize.net's secure hosted payment page, so your card number is never entered on or stored by our website.
  3. Invoices are due 7 days after they are issued.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover.

Important

We cannot and do not promise any particular result, score increase, or removal. Accurate, current, and verifiable information cannot be removed from a credit report. You have the right to dispute inaccurate information in your credit report yourself, directly with the credit bureaus, at no cost, and to get free credit reports at AnnualCreditReport.com.
