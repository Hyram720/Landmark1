Credit Ledger Pricing

$0.00 charged at signup or enrollment. Under the federal Credit Repair Organizations Act (CROA, 15 U.S.C. § 1679b(b)), Credit Ledger never charges any fee in advance before services are performed. You are billed only after (1) your mandatory 3-business-day cancellation window has expired and (2) the specific service on your invoice has been fully performed.

Credit Ledger (myledgercredit.com) is a consumer credit dispute and Metro 2 audit service operated by Sun Marketing Enterprises LLC, 2435 Patton St, Camden, NJ 08104.

What is always included at no extra charge

  • Your initial online credit consultation
  • Credit file disclosure requests (requests for a copy of your file)
  • Goodwill adjustment request letters
  • Help preparing a consumer complaint narrative for you to review and file yourself
  • Tracking of bureau and creditor statutory response deadlines (30/45-day FCRA clock)

Limits that protect you

  • $0.00 charged at signup: No fee is charged when you submit your enrollment or during your 3-business-day cancellation window.
  • 12-month maximum agreement term: Each Credit Services Agreement runs for up to 12 months and terminates automatically unless you sign a new written agreement.
  • Maximum total cap ($1,188): Your total charges under any Credit Services Agreement will not exceed $1,188 during a 12-month agreement term (enforced automatically by our server).
  • No cancellation penalty: You may cancel future services at any time with $0 cancellation fee. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

How payment works

  1. When a service is complete (your Week-1 Initial Credit Audit & Setup, a completed membership period, or a confirmed bureau deletion), we email you an itemized invoice describing the work performed and the date it was completed.
  2. Each invoice includes a secure payment link (/pay/:token) that opens Authorize.net's Accept Hosted payment page (connected to our approved credit-repair ISO: Soar Payments, PaymentCloud, Durango Merchant Services, or eCrypt). Your card number is never entered on or stored by our website.
  3. Invoices are due 7 days after they are issued. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover (cards only; eCheck is disabled per Authorize.net credit-repair rules).

Important disclosures

We cannot and do not promise any particular result, score increase, or removal. Accurate, current, and verifiable information cannot be removed from a credit report. You have the right to dispute inaccurate information in your credit report yourself, directly with the credit bureaus, at no cost, and to get free credit reports at AnnualCreditReport.com.
