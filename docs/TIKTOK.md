# TikTok: organic growth playbook (no ad spend)

**Goal:** build an audience of pet owners and turn views into orders through the link in the bio.
Every link carries `utm_source=tiktok`, so TikTok orders show up as TikTok in the admin page.

**Content pack:** `tiktok-content-pack.zip` (download link in the chat; about 150 MB)
- `videos/01–12`: before-and-after reveal videos, about 9 seconds each, 1080×1920
- `videos/13–15`: 13–15 second cut-downs of the infomercial
- `videos/16`: the full 51-second infomercial
- `videos/17`: "Same dog, 6 portraits. Which one wins?" (written to get comments)
- `photo-carousel/`: 12 portrait JPEGs for a TikTok photo post

**Schedule:** `marketing/tiktok-calendar.csv` has 30 days of posts. Each post has its file, the hook text, a ready-to-paste caption and the toggles to set.

---

## 1. Set up the account (15 minutes, on your phone)

1. Install TikTok and sign up with a **new email** for the brand, such as studio@ or your Gmail. Keep it separate from any personal account.
2. **Username:** try `custompetportraitstudio` first, then `petportraitstudio` or `paintmypet`.
3. **Name:** Custom Pet Portrait Studio
4. **Profile photo:** Khabba's Royal portrait. Use `photo-carousel/01-khabba-royal.jpg` and crop it to the face.
5. **Bio:**
   > Your pet, painted like royalty 👑
   > 1 photo → 4 portraits in minutes
   > Canvas shipped 🇺🇸🇨🇦 · 20% off launch ⬇️
6. **Switch to a Business account:** Settings → Account → *Switch to Business Account* → category *Art & Crafts*. It's free, and it unlocks analytics.
7. **Website link:** `custompetportraitstudio.com/tiktok`. If TikTok doesn't offer the website field yet (new accounts sometimes have to wait until 1,000 followers), do two things meanwhile:
   - put `custompetportraitstudio.com` in the bio text
   - pin a comment with the link on every video
8. **Warm up the account before your first post:** spend 10–15 minutes scrolling and liking pet videos (#dogsoftiktok, #petportrait). This teaches TikTok who the account is for.

## 2. Posting rules

- **Frequency:** 2 posts a day for the first 14 days (12pm and 7pm ET), then 1 a day. Posting consistently matters more than any single video.
- **Post from the phone app and add a trending sound:**
  1. Open the video in TikTok.
  2. Tap *Sounds*.
  3. Pick something from *Trending* and set its volume low.
  The videos are exported silent on purpose, so the sound you add doesn't clash with anything.
- **Turn on two toggles for every post** (under *More options*):
  - **AI-generated content: ON.** TikTok requires a label on realistic AI images. The portraits are AI-painted, and the non-Khabba "before" photos are AI-generated sample pets. Labeling them protects the account from strikes.
  - **Content disclosure → Your brand: ON.** You're promoting your own business.
- **Be honest in captions:**
  - Khabba is your real dog, so "my dog" is true for his videos.
  - For the sample pets (Bella, Luna, Bruno, Max, Ginger, Daisy), never say "my dog" or "a customer's dog". Use "POV", "sample" or the pet's name.
- **Cover text:** pick a frame with the hook text visible as the cover.

## 3. Engagement routine (20 minutes a day)

1. **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** That window drives reach.
2. **Answer questions with a video reply.** When someone asks "does it work for cats?" or "how much?", tap *Reply with video*. Use one of the reveal videos, or film your phone showing the website.
3. **The "comment 1–6" video (#17):** reply to voters with the matching portrait.
4. **Comment on 10 big pet accounts a day** in your niche. Be genuine and funny, and never paste links. Early comments on big posts bring profile visits.
5. **When customers post their portraits**, ask to *Stitch* or *Duet* them, or repost them with their permission. Real customer content beats everything else.

## 4. What to watch (weekly, in TikTok Analytics)

| Metric | Good | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Average watch time on a 9-second reveal | 6s or more | Keep the hook style |
| Video gets fewer than 300 views | — | Normal at the start. Re-post the best ones after 2 weeks with a new sound and hook. |
| One video gets 10× your usual views | — | Make 3 more in the same format within 48 hours |
| Profile views → website clicks | 5% or more | If lower, rewrite the bio's first line |

TikTok orders appear as `tiktok` in the order source on `/admin`.

## 5. Next steps once it's moving

- **"Comment your pet's breed and I'll paint one" videos.** Paint the most-liked breed and post it as a reply. It's the highest-engagement format in this niche. Each painting costs about $0.20–0.50 in OpenAI credits.
- **TikTok Shop:** list the digital portrait and canvas there once the account is eligible. Affiliates then promote the products for a commission, still with no ad spend.
- **Repurpose every video** to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels the same day. It's the same file, and the reach is free.
