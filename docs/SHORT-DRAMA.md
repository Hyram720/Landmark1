# Short drama series: *The Pound Prince*

A 1-minute-per-episode animated pet drama for a new YouTube channel (and TikTok / Reels).
Format based on the "short drama" pattern: open on a shock, a twist in every scene, end on a cliffhanger.

- **Style:** classic 3D family animation (Higgsfield Short Drama → "Classic 3D")
- **Aspect:** vertical 9:16
- **Length:** ~60 seconds per episode, 2 clips per episode
- **Draft first:** generate at 480p, pick the best take, upscale only the winners to 1080p

## How the series markets the store (without being an ad)

The plot runs on a **royal oil portrait of a dog**, which is exactly what The Royal style sells. The story never
stops to pitch. The pitch is:

1. **2-second end card after each cliffhanger** (after the story, so it doesn't hurt watch time):
   *"Part 2 tomorrow 👑 Paint your pet like Biscuit → custompetportraitstudio.com/biscuit"*
2. **Pinned comment + description** on every episode with the same link.
3. **Make Biscuit's real portrait with our own Royal style** and post it as a Short:
   "We painted Biscuit for real." That's the proof the product exists.
4. Tracking: `/biscuit` and `/yt` redirect to `/offer?utm_source=youtube&utm_campaign=pound-prince`,
   so orders from the series show up separately from TikTok.

Note: links inside a YouTube Short aren't clickable, so say/print the short URL; the clickable link lives
in the pinned comment, description and channel header.

## Characters

| Name | Who | Look (for the character image) |
|---|---|---|
| **Biscuit** | Scruffy rescue terrier mix, ignored at the shelter | Wiry tan fur, **one white ear**, big brown eyes, frayed red collar |
| **Margot** | Shelter volunteer, mid-20s, Biscuit's only friend | Green shelter hoodie, messy bun, paint-stained fingers (art student) |
| **Mr. Pell** | Estate lawyer, dry and precise | Grey suit, round glasses, leather briefcase |
| **Victor Ashford** | Greedy nephew of the late Mrs. Ashford | Slick hair, camel coat, gold signet ring |
| **Duke** | Victor's pampered white poodle | Groomed white poodle, diamond collar |

**Props:** the Royal portrait (Biscuit-lookalike in crown, ermine robe, red velvet throne, gold frame), the
second "fake" portrait (Duke in a crown), Biscuit's frayed collar with an old brass tag, a tablet for the
video will.

**Locations:** county animal shelter (kennel row, lobby), Ashford Manor gallery hall.

## Episode 1: "Passed Over"

**Clip 1.1: Nobody picks him** *(shelter kennel row, day)*

> A family walks past kennel after kennel. They stop at Biscuit's. He wags. They keep walking.
>
> **SHELTER MANAGER (O.S.):** Last call. Anyone not adopted by Friday... goes out the back.
>
> **MARGOT** (kneeling at the bars, whispering): Not you. I won't let it be you.
>
> The lobby door BANGS open. **MR. PELL** enters carrying something large wrapped in a cloth.
>
> **MR. PELL:** I'm looking for a dog. One white ear.

**Clip 1.2: The painting** *(shelter lobby)*

> Pell pulls off the cloth: a **royal oil portrait**. A crowned dog on a velvet throne, wiry tan fur, **one white ear**.
> Margot looks at the painting. Looks at Biscuit. Identical.
>
> **MR. PELL:** Mrs. Eleanor Ashford left four million dollars... to the dog in this painting.
>
> **MARGOT:** Biscuit?!
>
> The door BANGS again. **VICTOR** strides in with **DUKE** on a leash, and a second wrapped canvas.
>
> **VICTOR:** That mutt is NOT the dog in the painting.
>
> He rips off the cloth: **another royal portrait**: Duke, wearing the same crown.
>
> **VICTOR:** *This* is my aunt's dog.
>
> Close on both paintings side by side. **SUPER: "One of these paintings is a fake."**
>
> *[END CARD, 2s]*

## Episode 2: "Wet Paint"

**Clip 2.1: The deadline** *(shelter lobby, continuous)*

> **MR. PELL:** The court compares both portraits Friday. Until then, the dog stays here.
>
> **SHELTER MANAGER:** Friday is his last day.
>
> **MARGOT** (grabbing the adoption form): Then he's not staying here. I'm adopting him. Right now.
>
> Victor smirks and leaves. As he passes, Margot brushes Duke's portrait with her sleeve. She stops. Looks at her fingers.
>
> Gold paint. Still **wet**.
>
> **MARGOT** (to herself): Paintings from 1990 don't smudge.

**Clip 2.2: The collar** *(Margot's tiny apartment, night)*

> Biscuit sleeps at the foot of the bed. Margot photographs the smudge as evidence.
>
> A window CREAKS. A gloved hand reaches in. Biscuit jolts awake, barking. The hand is gone, and so is **Biscuit's collar**.
>
> Margot runs to the window. Empty street. On the sill: the collar's broken clasp.
>
> **MARGOT:** Why would anyone steal a collar?
>
> Knock at the door. She opens it. **VICTOR**, holding the collar up. A brass tag swings from it, engraved **"E.A."**
>
> **VICTOR:** Looking for this?
>
> *[END CARD, 2s]*

## Episode 3: "The Unveiling"

**Clip 3.1: Proof** *(Ashford Manor gallery hall, Friday)*

> Both portraits on easels. Mr. Pell presides. Victor holds up the collar.
>
> **VICTOR:** Eleanor Ashford's initials. Found on *my* dog's collar.
>
> **MARGOT:** Found *through my window* at midnight.
>
> Gasps. Pell raises a hand. Biscuit pulls free, trots to the real portrait, and sniffs the **back of the frame**.
>
> Margot turns the canvas around. Pressed into the old paint: **a small paw print**.
>
> **MARGOT:** She had him stand on it. The artist's signature.
>
> Biscuit lifts his paw against it. **Perfect match.** Duke's paw is twice the size.

**Clip 3.2: The will** *(continuous)*

> **MR. PELL:** There's one more thing. Mrs. Ashford left a video.
>
> On his tablet, frail old **ELEANOR** smiles into the camera.
>
> **ELEANOR (ON SCREEN):** If you're watching this, Victor tried to take it. Biscuit, my love, go home with whoever saved you.
>
> Biscuit climbs into Margot's lap. Victor storms out. Margot laughs and cries at once.
>
> **MR. PELL** (closing the tablet, quietly): There's a problem. Mrs. Ashford had a *second* pet.
>
> Slow push in: a third covered painting in the corner. The cloth slips. A **cat**, in a tiara.
>
> **SUPER: "Season 2: The Duchess."**
>
> *[END CARD, 3s]*

## Why these beats (the patterns from the video)

- **Shock in the first 3 seconds:** "goes out the back," a stranger asking for a dog, a gloved hand.
- **A twist every clip:** lookalike portrait → rival portrait → wet paint → stolen collar → paw print → second pet.
- **Underdog + inheritance + villain relative:** three of the most reused short-drama tropes.
- **Cliffhanger on a reveal line, not a fade:** "Looking for this?"
- **Season hook** sets up a cat series (*The Duchess*, our Duchess style), so cat owners are targeted next.

## Making it

1. Use the Higgsfield **short drama** workflow: paste one episode's script, style "Classic 3D," vertical.
2. Generate each character image first; reuse the same images for all 3 episodes so they stay consistent.
3. 480p drafts, 2–3 takes per clip, upscale the best take to 1080p.
4. Edit: tight cut, subtitles, name cards; add the end card.
5. Budget from the reference video: ~1,000–1,500 credits per episode at 480p draft + upscale.
