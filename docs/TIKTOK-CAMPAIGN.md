## The big idea: "We paint one pet a day, free"

Every post invites people to **comment their pet's name and breed**. Each day the team picks one commenter and paints their pet for free. The winner's reveal becomes the next day's video.

Why it works:
- **Comments are the #1 signal TikTok uses** to push a video to more people. "Comment to win" gets 5–20× more comments than a normal post.
- **Every winner is new content.** You never run out of videos.
- **Winners share their own portrait**, and those posts reach their followers for free.
- **Real pets build trust** that sample pets can't.

### Winner workflow (about 5 minutes a day)
1. Pick a winner from yesterday's comments. Choose an interesting breed, or a funny comment.
2. Reply to their comment:
   > "You won today's free portrait! 🎨 DM us a clear photo of [pet name] and your email."
3. When the DM arrives, go to **admin page → 🎁 Gift a free portrait**. Upload the photo, pick the style, and type the name, their email and a note such as "TikTok winner @user".
4. Portraits arrive in about 5 minutes. Screen-record their gallery page and post it with **"Reply to [their comment]"**:
   > "Today's free portrait goes to Biscuit the corgi 👑"
5. Ask them to post it and tag you:
   > "We'd love to see it on your page, so tag us!"

**Rules to put in the pinned comment** (keep giveaways clean):
> No purchase necessary. One free digital portrait per day, picked from comments. 18+, US & Canada. Not sponsored by TikTok.

1. **The reveal:** phone photo, then a flash, then the portrait. Pack videos #01–12.
2. **Reaction:** film a pet looking at its own portrait on your laptop or phone. Real pets, no AI needed.
3. **"Rate my dog's portraits":** swipe through the 6 styles and rate each one.

5. **Winner reply:** "Reply to @user": "Today's free portrait goes to…"

7. **Memorial (gentle, once a week at most):** "For the pets we miss." Use the watercolor style. Never be pushy about it.

## Creator outreach (week 3)

**Find them:** search #dogsoftiktok, #catsoftiktok and #[breed]oftiktok. Pick accounts with **5k–50k followers** whose videos get lots of comments. Smaller accounts reply to DMs and their followers trust them.

**DM template:**
> Hi! We're a small pet-portrait studio and we love [pet name]'s videos 😂 We'd like to paint [pet name] as royalty for free: 4 museum-style portraits, no strings attached. If you love them and want to share, amazing, but no obligation. Want one? Just send a clear photo and your email!

When they send the photo, use **🎁 Gift a free portrait** in the admin page, with the note "creator @username". If they post it, comment, stitch it, and thank them publicly.

**Tip:** if they post, offer them a personal code their followers can use. Ask Claude to create one, such as `BISCUIT15`. It takes about 2 minutes and is tracked in Stripe.
