Questions

How fast will I get my portrait?

Digital portraits usually arrive by email within minutes of checkout. Canvas prints ship in 3–5 business days after you choose your favorite in your gallery.

How does the canvas order work?

You pay for the canvas at checkout, and the 4 digital portraits are included. When they're ready, open your private gallery and tap the one you want printed. We send that one to print and ship it to the address you gave at checkout (US and Canada).

What if I don't love it?

You get 4 portraits to choose from. If none feel right, use your one free repaint from your gallery within 7 days (before printing) and we'll make 4 new ones, in the same style or a different one.

What if the name is misspelled?

The nameplate shows the name exactly as you type it. If you made a typo, fix it on your gallery page and all portraits update in seconds, any time before printing.

What resolution are the files?

Every portrait is 2400×3600 pixels at 300 DPI (8×12 inches). Canvas prints are produced at the exact canvas size at 300 DPI.

Can I order as a gift?

Yes. Use your own email, then forward the gallery link, or ship the canvas straight to them by entering their address at checkout.

What's the Christmas deadline?

For a canvas delivered in the US before Christmas, order by December 10 and choose your favorite portrait the same day. Digital portraits usually arrive within minutes, so there's no deadline for those.

Do you do memorial portraits?

Yes. Many customers honor a pet who has passed. Any clear photo works, even an older one.

How do I get the launch discount?

While the launch offer runs, it's applied for you when you check out from this site. There's no code to type.
