Questions

How fast will I get my portrait?

Digital portraits usually arrive within minutes of checkout. Canvas prints ship in 3–5 business days after you pick your favorite.

What resolution are the files?

Every portrait is 2400×3600 pixels at 300 DPI (8×12 inches). Canvas prints are produced at the exact canvas size at 300 DPI.

What if the name is misspelled?

The name is engraved exactly as you type it. If you made a typo, fix it on your gallery page and all portraits update in seconds.

What if I don't love it?

You get 4 versions to choose from. If none feel right, use your one free repaint from your gallery within 7 days (before printing) and we'll paint 4 new ones, in the same style or a different one.

Can I order as a gift?

Absolutely — use your own email, then forward the gallery link or ship the canvas straight to them.

Do you do memorial portraits?

Yes. Many customers honor a pet who has passed. Any clear photo works, even an older one.
