How your portrait is made

Your portraits are made with AI image generation, not drawn by hand.

When you order, your photo and the style you picked go to an AI image model, which creates 4 portraits. We then enlarge each one for download, add your pet's name on a nameplate if you asked for one, and place the results in your private gallery.

Enlarging adds pixels, not new detail, so the files suit phones, screens and sharing. Canvas prints are made from the same image and have a soft, painterly look up close. Read the full answers in the FAQ or see About.

🔍 Sharp and close. Head and shoulders fill most of the frame, not blurry.
🐾 One pet in the shot. Our styles are built around a single pet.

Questions

How fast will I get my portrait?

Digital portraits usually arrive by email within minutes of checkout. Canvas prints ship in 3–5 business days after you choose your favorite in your gallery.

How does the canvas order work?

You pay for the canvas at checkout, and the 4 digital portraits are included. When they're ready, open your private gallery and tap the one you want printed. We send that one to print and ship it to the address you gave at checkout (US and Canada).

What if a digital file is defective or missing?

Digital files are final sale. Contact studio support for a replacement if a file is defective or was not delivered.

What if the name is misspelled?

The nameplate shows the name exactly as you type it. If you made a typo, fix it on your gallery page and all portraits update in seconds, any time before printing.

What size are the files?

Each portrait is painted at 1024×1536 pixels and then upscaled for download and print. Upscaling adds pixels, not new detail. That is great for phones, screens and sharing, and it prints sharpest at small sizes, up to about 3.4×5.1 inches, and still looks good up to about 6.8×10.2 inches. Canvas prints are made from the same image, so up close they have a soft, painterly look rather than fine photo detail.

Can I order as a gift?

Yes. Use your own email, then forward the gallery link, or ship the canvas straight to them by entering their address at checkout.

What's the Christmas deadline?

Digital portraits usually arrive by email within minutes. Canvas prints usually ship 3–5 business days after you choose a portrait. Carrier arrival varies. The Christmas canvas order-by date is pending fulfillment confirmation.

Do you do memorial portraits?

Yes. Many customers honor a pet who has passed. Any clear photo works, even an older one.
