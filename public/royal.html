    Questions

    What does the Royal style look like?

    Your pet is painted as a Renaissance-style oil portrait: a jeweled gold crown, an ermine-trimmed royal robe, a medallion and a red velvet throne, in warm golden light.

    How fast will I get my portrait?

    Digital portraits usually arrive within minutes of checkout. Canvas prints ship in 3–5 business days after you pick your favorite.

    What resolution are the files?

    Every portrait is 2400×3600 pixels at 300 DPI (8×12 inches). Canvas prints are produced at the exact canvas size at 300 DPI.

    What if I don't love it?

    You get 4 versions to choose from. If none feel right, use your one free repaint from your gallery within 7 days (before printing) and we'll paint 4 new ones.

    Which phones does the royal case fit?

    The Tough case is on Etsy for all eight iPhone 16 and iPhone 17 models, and in a separate listing for the Samsung Galaxy S25, S25+, S26 and S26+ (Ultra models aren't offered). Pick your model on the Etsy listing and double-check it, because custom cases can't be exchanged for a different model.

    What is the royal blanket like?

    It's a Silky Throw, 50" x 60": soft silk-touch polyester printed edge to edge on one side with a white back, and machine washable. Your pet's royal portrait sits on a deep burgundy background with a gold border and a gold nameplate with your pet's name.
