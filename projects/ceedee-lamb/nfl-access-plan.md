# Getting official NFL / Cowboys footage access

**Goal:** earn legitimate access to real NFL footage (or Cowboys behind-the-scenes access), so Sun
Custom Designs can edit actual CeeDee Lamb plays without copyright strikes.

## The reality (as of October 2026)
- **NFL creator programs are invite-only.** No public application exists. For 2026 the NFL brought back
  **Creator of the Week** with YouTube (behind-the-scenes access to teams, players, and gamedays,
  now across 30 clubs), plus a Snapchat creator program, a female creator partnership, and a Celebrity
  Photographer Series ([SVG](https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/09/10/nfl-brings-back-creator-programs-to-deliver-access-original-storytelling-during-the-2026-season/),
  [LBB](https://lbbonline.com/news/NFL-Creator-Programmes-Return-for-2026-Season)). The partners are
  large creators like Deestroying and Zach King
  ([Yahoo](https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/nfl-partners-deestroying-zach-king-130000429.html)).
- **Access Pass** (licensed NFL archive footage that creators can monetize on YouTube) was reported in
  2023 as being for a select group of creators connected to a team's city. It was YouTube-only and
  excluded live game footage ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/the-nfl-gives-creators-access-to-its-archives-for-content-creation-with-pre-approved-footage/)).
  This is the program that would let you edit real CeeDee plays.
- **TikTok's 2026 NFL deal** puts highlights on the NFL's own TikTok accounts. It doesn't give creators
  footage ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/09/02/tiktok-2026-nfl-season-game-plan-partnership/)).
- **The Cowboys are actively buying creator content:** a 2026 multi-year deal with **Victory+** for
  "socially focused and creator-driven" Cowboys content
  ([SVG](https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/04/17/victory-and-dallas-cowboys-announce-multi-year-content-streaming-partnership/)),
  and they back **GameSquare**, a Frisco creator media company
  ([YPulse](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2024/08/09/gamesquare-a-gaming-and-entertainment-firm-backed-by-the-dallas-cowboys-is-launching-a-new-football-show-called-nfl-4-the-fans-live/)).
  **These are the most realistic doors.**

## The ladder
### Phase 1: Build proof (now → 90 days)
Invites go to creators with an audience and a track record, so build both:
- Post the **Running CeeDee's Routes** series plus other original Cowboys-fan content, 1 per day, in the
  60s payout format.
- Targets: **10k followers and 100k views per 30 days**. That's Creator Rewards eligibility, and the
  minimum credibility for a pitch.
- Keep a **media kit** (a one-page PDF or Notion page): follower count, 30-day views, average watch
  time, % US / % Texas audience, your top 3 videos, and Sun Custom Designs' editing style. TikTok
  analytics has everything except the style samples.
- Tag `@dallascowboys` and `@ceedee` on the recreation episodes. Reposts from those accounts are the
  fastest way to get noticed.

### Phase 2: The Cowboys ecosystem (start at ~10k followers)
| Who | Why | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| **Victory+** (Cowboys content partner) | They need creator-driven Cowboys content for 2026 | Contract editing, or a creator slot in their Cowboys programming |
| **GameSquare** (Frisco, Cowboys-backed) | Runs creator-led football shows | Freelance editing or creator roster |
| **Cowboys digital/social team** | Runs creator activations | Gameday creator access, a feature, a repost |
| **CeeDee Lamb's marketing team** | Pro athletes run brand content, camps, and youth events | Film and edit his camp or community events, or a collab on the recreation series |

### Phase 3: The NFL (with 50k+ followers and Cowboys work to show)
- Pitch the NFL's social/creator team for **Creator of the Week** or **Access Pass**, with Dallas as the
  home market. Lead with your best performing videos and the fact that it's all original or licensed
  content.
- Monitor NFL Communications for new creator programs:
  [media.nfl.com](https://media.nfl.com/news-and-releases/corporate-communications/creators-return-as-hallmark-of-nfl-season-bringing-unrivaled-con).

## What not to do
- Don't repost NFL broadcast clips "to build the audience first." Strikes and muted videos hurt the
  account the NFL would look at, and reposted clips earn nothing from Creator Rewards anyway.
- Don't use NFL or Cowboys logos in your branding, or imply a partnership before one exists.
