# LEGACY LANE — a LegacyReady Family soap-opera series

**Format:** short-form social soap (Reels / TikTok / Shorts 9:16, FB feed 4:5), 30–60s episodes, 4–6 scenes each.
**Promise of the show:** Every episode, the Whitmore family pays the price for "nobody knew where anything was" — and someone finds clarity with LegacyReady Family.
**Recurring end card (every episode):** *"Preserve your family's past. Protect your family's future. Start free at LegacyReadyFamily.com — no credit card required."*
**Recurring open:** dramatic organ sting, slow push-in on the Whitmore house on a rainy evening, title card **LEGACY LANE** in gold serif script.

**Compliance:** Fiction, played for melodrama. LegacyReady is not a law firm and gives no legal, tax, financial, or insurance advice — VO stays soft ("organize", "keep it where your family can find it"), never promises outcomes.

## Production pipeline (Higgsfield)

1. **Character sheets** — generate the 5 core sheets (+ Harold, + guest as needed) with `generate_image`, 16:9. Approve each; these are the series' source of truth.
2. **Scene stills** — for every scene, `generate_image` with the relevant sheets attached as reference images + the scene shot description. Keep the same house/living-room description across episodes for set consistency.
3. **Animate** — `generate_video` image-to-video from each approved still (5–8s, 9:16).
4. **Voice & music** — `generate_audio` for VO lines and the organ sting; reuse the same sting every episode.
5. **Assemble** — stitch scenes + end card into one episode with burned-in captions.
