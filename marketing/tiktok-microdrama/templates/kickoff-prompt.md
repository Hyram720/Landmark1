# Kickoff prompt (paste into the Claude app → Code tab, inside your show folder)

```
I want to make a vertical AI micro-drama series for TikTok, one episode a day.
My starting idea: <one sentence, e.g. "a cleaner falsely accused of stealing a ring is secretly the owner's long-lost daughter">.

Before writing anything, ask me questions one at a time: genre, tone,
setting/places, who the show is for, episode length, main characters,
and the film look. Give me 3–4 options for each so I can just pick.

Then:
1. Save my answers as show-brief.md (use templates/show-brief.md).
2. Plan a 5-episode season in season-outline.md. Every episode starts
   with a hook and ends on a cliffhanger.
3. Check Episode 1 against story-rules.md and show me the table.
4. List every person, place and prop that appears more than once, and
   make a reference sheet for each in sheets/ (use
   templates/character-sheet-prompt.md).
5. Create show-notes.md from templates/show-notes.md.

After that, I'll just say "make episode N" and you'll follow the
episode skill in .claude/skills/episode/.
```
