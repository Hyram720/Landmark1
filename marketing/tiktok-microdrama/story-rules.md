# The 5 Story Rules

Check every episode against these rules before anything is drawn. Show a table with one row per beat and one column per rule (✅ / ❌ plus a short note). Rewrite **only** the beats that fail.

1. **Question first.** The first 2 seconds open on a moment that makes the viewer ask "why?" Don't answer it until later.
2. **Make us care.** Early on, the lead does something good or fair and is treated badly for it.
3. **The viewer knows more.** At least once, the audience sees a danger, lie or secret that the lead can't see.
4. **Every beat changes direction.** Each beat flips the situation (good→bad or bad→good). Beats connect with "BUT" or "SO," never "and then." If a beat changes nothing, cut it.
5. **Payoff, then reopen.** Near the end, give the win the viewer was hoping for, then raise a bigger question and **stop**. The last line is the cliffhanger. (On TikTok Series this is where the paywall goes.)

## Extra checks for TikTok
- Is the first spoken line the strongest line in the episode?
- Can someone follow the story **on mute** from the captions alone?
- Does the last frame clearly tell people to watch the next part?
- Does it fit within 60–90 seconds (about 13 shots × 5 s)?
