## Claims check

- The find is called **"a lead"**, matching the site's guidance that a matching record is a research lead, not proof.
- No income claims and no testimonials. No third-party genealogy brands are named.
- The actors are AI-generated, and the end card says so.

## How it was made

- **Keyframes:** Seedream 5.0 Flash, using the first image as a reference so Dana looks the same in every shot. A couple of early stills came from Higgsfield Soul 2.
- **Animation:** shot 1 is Veo 3.1 Lite. Shots 2–7 are Seedance 1.5 Pro, 8 seconds each with native dialogue.
- **Assembly:** done in the Higgsfield sandbox with ffmpeg. Caption timings come from transcribing each shot.
- **Shot 1:** the handwriting garbles after about 2 seconds, so the shot cuts to a slow push-in on a clean frame.
- **Shot 3:** its generated audio was unusable, so it has room tone and a caption instead.
- **Shot 7:** starts 2.5 seconds in, after the frame has turned toward her, and holds the final wide shot.

## Notes

- On this free account, Veo 3.1 Lite blocks every photoreal face used as a start image; it reports them as `nsfw` and refunds the credits. Use Seedance 1.5 Pro (about 9.6 credits per 8 seconds) for realistic actors.
- Photos on screen must face the character holding them. Shots 2, 4 and 6 were re-shot for this, following Fred's review.
