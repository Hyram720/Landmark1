# Sports TikTok viral playbook

What the For You algorithm rewards, roughly in order: **completion rate → rewatches → shares → comments
→ likes → follows from the video**. Early performance with a small test audience decides whether a
video gets pushed wider, so the first 1–2 seconds and the first hour after posting matter most.

## Hook formulas (on-screen text, ≤ 7 words)
Curiosity gap. The viewer has to keep watching to resolve it:
- "Watch #12 on the left 👀" (directs the eye and promises something)
- "Wait for the defender's reaction"
- "Nobody saw this pass coming"
- "This is a 9th grader"
- "Ref had no choice"
- "The ending is crazy" / "Keep watching" (overused; save for genuinely late payoffs)
- "Rate this move 1–10" (drives comments)
- "Is this the best catch in [state] this year?" (local pride drives shares)
- "POV: you're the defender"

Rules: put it on screen at frame 1, never start on a scoreboard or a dead ball, and don't spoil the payoff
in the hook.

## Edit patterns that work for sports
| Pattern | Use for | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| **Play → slow-mo replay** | Any single big play | Real speed (4–7s) → 0.4x replay with 1.4x zoom → reaction |
| **Cold open on the payoff** | Dunks, KOs, goals | 1s flash of the payoff → cut back to the build-up → full play |
| **Rapid-fire mixtape** | Recruiting reels, season recaps | 6–12 clips of 1.5–3s each, cut on the beat or the impact, best clip first and last |
| **Spot the detail** | Skill / IQ plays | Hook points to a detail, zoom/replay reveals it |
| **Reaction stack** | Crowd / bench moments | Play → crowd → bench → player celebration |
| **Before → after** | Training content | Old rep vs. new rep, side by side or back-to-back |

Pacing: change something on screen every 1.5–3 seconds (a cut, zoom, caption, or speed change). Dead
air kills completion.

## Captions on screen
- 2–5 words per line, bold sans, white with a thick black outline, yellow for emphasis.
- Lower-middle of the frame, above TikTok's caption area. The renderer handles placement.
- Write them like a hype commentator, not a narrator: "ONE MAN TO BEAT", "HE'S GONE", "OH NO 😭".

## TikTok post caption
`[short hook line or context] + [question that invites an answer]`
- "15-year-old point guard from Camden 🔥 Who's guarding him?"
- "Best goal of our season so far. Top bins or lucky? 🤔"

## Hashtags (3–5 total)
Mix: 1 sport-broad + 1–2 niche + 1 local/team + optionally 1 format tag.
- Broad: `#basketball #football #soccer #baseball #volleyball #wrestling`
- Niche: `#hoopmixtape #highschoolfootball #fyp` (sparingly), `#ankletaker #goalkeeper #walkoff`
- Local/team: `#njhoops #[school]athletics #[city]sports`
- Format: `#sportsedit #highlights #recruiting`
Skip walls of 20+ hashtags. Relevance helps the algorithm categorize the video, and volume doesn't.

## Sound
- Original game audio (crowd, commentary, the swish, the hit) is often the best sound for sports.
- Trending sounds: add them **in the TikTok app** at low volume under the original audio so the post shows
  up on the sound's page. Use the "commercial" sound library for business or brand accounts.

## Posting
- Consistency beats volume: 1–2 posts a day if you can sustain it, otherwise 3–5 a week.
- Typical strong windows (viewer's local time): 6–9am, 12–2pm, 7–11pm. Check TikTok Analytics →
  Followers → activity hours, which is the real answer for each account.
- Post game highlights **within hours** of the game. Recency drives local shares.
- Reply to comments in the first 60 minutes. Reply-with-video to a good comment makes a free second post.
- Re-cut winners: if a clip pops, post a new edit of the same moment with a different hook 2–3 days later.

## Specs
1080x1920, 9:16, 30fps (60 if the source is 60), H.264 High, AAC 48kHz, under 10 minutes and under 287 MB
for mobile upload. Upload from the TikTok app or desktop at full quality; avoid re-saving through
messaging apps, which recompresses the video.

## Rights checklist
- Own footage, your athletes (with parent consent for minors), or licensed: ✅
- Network or league broadcast clips: high risk of mutes, takedowns, and strikes. Recommend filming
  originals or transformative commentary instead. Never help evade detection.
